# Sandbox execution and financial readiness

The sandbox uses two independent decisions. `pre_execution_ready` authorizes consideration of a new stop; it does not establish realised savings. The readiness API reports `READY` or `BLOCKED`, with a separate `verification_status`. A frozen, complete baseline can be execution-ready while verification is `MEASURING`. Operational safety, exact-plan approval and fresh provider preflight still apply.

The [trusted SLO increment](slo-safety.md), validated in local fixtures, adds `slo_status: CLEAR` and exact objective ID/version/hash plus immutable evaluation ID/hash to schema-3 readiness and signed plans. A health evaluation alone is insufficient: the complete singleton stop/restoration window must be outside explicitly required availability. A worker-recorded violation disqualifies the entire signed measurement; the API appends a null blocked correction without changing prior claim history. Later good telemetry cannot revive that measurement. The schema-2 fixture/history path remains available, while every new live stop requires the current schema-3 safety authority.

## Current evidence version 3

Version 3 adds source receipts and an immutable proof set to the baseline, measurement and signed ChangePlan. The ingester resolves registered AWS Data Exports revisions, versioned objects, native invoices and payer-wide commitments. Independent activity ingress resolves registered machine identities and immutable receipts. API request amounts, uploaded invoices, workload samples and submitted completeness flags cannot establish this authority. Database receipt provenance must be checked before the private economics worker receives a proof; a receipt identifier by itself is not authentication.

The additive `/v3/normalize` mapping chooses native `NetUnblendedCost`, including an explicit zero, or `UnblendedCost` when net pricing is absent. Its canonical pricing basis is `NET_UNBLENDED` or `UNBLENDED`; it does not synthesize `ContractedCost`. `/v3/evaluate` and schema-version-3 requests to `/v2/evaluate` validate original provider fields against canonical identity and exact decimal amounts. Version-2 routes and historical data remain available for their existing contract.

Execution readiness requires exactly fourteen complete local calendar days of actual, nonoverlapping compute billing coverage, with the latest complete day ending no more than 48 hours before activation. Activity receipts must cover the same period, retain one immutable metric definition, include heartbeats and preserve accepted demand as the unit. Accepted, completed, rejected, backlog and inflight values are checked independently. Scheduled idle windows must have explicit zero values and clear incident/deployment/job signals. Missing observations remain unknown. Full payer/all-linked-account/all-region commitment inventory must establish zero RI and Savings Plan exposure; resource-local or partial snapshots cannot prove this. Covered portfolios require a later allocation model.

Current inventory counts do not erase history. Native RI/SP coverage, fees, negations or ARNs overlapping either evidence period anywhere in the full payer source block this bounded model with `HISTORICAL_COMMITMENT_EXPOSURE`, including commitments retired before invoice collection. Unallocated native credit/refund/discount adjustments or negative portfolio lines in the period block with `UNALLOCATED_FINANCIAL_ADJUSTMENT`. Reconciliation alone cannot allocate a global discount to the target's avoidable price. This conservative pilot does not infer allocation from an invoice total or assume that net pricing on some target rows resolves all payer adjustments.

Measurement starts after activation and requires at least fourteen complete local calendar days. Weekday/time windows use the complete baseline's matching observations, while full-day accepted/completed work must remain preserved and rejection/queue measures must not regress. Startup compute through the required ready time remains an actual cost. Retained EBS/IP costs receive no avoided-cost credit; increased retained rates or other incremental charges require source-backed attribution to owned execution and reduce savings. Unknown allocation blocks a verified result.

Both baseline and measurement retain `source_scope_records`: all canonical rows from every pinned revision, with row counts tied to each receipt. `invoice_scope_records` is the exact payer/entity/currency subset; every row must belong to the full source and all matching source rows must be included. Single-scope unit callers may omit `source_scope_records` and use invoice scope as the whole source. Hosted service snapshots always provide both. Associated EBS/IP charges often use a volume or address resource ID rather than the instance ID. The evaluator checks all account/region EC2 storage/IP lines in the authorized source, comparing rates per resource and native charge category. New or increased unallocated costs block with `ALLOCATION_SCOPE_REQUIRED`; unrelated account changes or retained charges in another currency/entity can conservatively block this pilot until a later allocation model resolves them. Filtered target-only billing is insufficient proof that related costs were preserved.

Billing observations have three states. `OBSERVED` references real native source records. `ABSENT_FINAL_EXPORT` is allowed only within executor-owned stopped intervals and a complete, finalized export receipt covering the interval, with no contradictory compute records. `UNKNOWN` supplies no actual cost. The system must not insert synthetic zero-cost CUR rows for stopped hours. Withdrawal of export completeness/finality turns formerly proved absence into unknown rather than zero.

Native issued invoices bind payer account, invoicing entity, base currency and billing month. Cancelled invoices and credit/payment documents cannot establish finality. Issuance must follow the whole billing month and precede the evaluation clock. The evaluator independently reconciles all canonical records for the payer/entity/currency invoice scope to native `BaseCurrencyAmount.TotalAmount` at 99.5% or better, and requires each target record to belong to that reconciled source. A filtered resource total, submitted reconciliation ratio, final flag, currency conversion or invoice from another payer cannot pass. Multiple source revisions/months retain separate receipt identities.

The current contract requires native `TaxAuthorityStatus: ISSUED`; a missing optional provider status remains provisional. Actual account invoice responses and issuance semantics must be validated before live verification is accepted. Native line IDs that repeat in disjoint authorized revisions are resolved by observation timestamps while financial rows retain their complete source/version/record identity.

Provisional, partial, cancelled or replaced evidence produces a nonverified correction with the same resource/time claim key. The ledger must append that correction, supersede the previous current amount and retain history. Cross-identity and canonical amount tampering are rejected. Annualised estimates remain separate from verified period amounts, and negative verified results remain visible.

The new contracts have local unit/HTTP coverage. The AWS source and activity integrations still require supervised validation against the configured customer accounts, real export deliveries, native invoice availability and genuine workload telemetry. No live customer has collected these periods or established verified savings in this repository's acceptance record.

## Historical evidence version 2

Historical version-2 actions used a sealed baseline, a reserved measurement identifier and a sealed readiness report. Their identifiers and activation timestamp remain signed into their immutable version-2 ChangePlan. This path is fixture/history-only; live STOP now requires version 3, while existing owned START authority remains valid. Baselines retain tenant, internal resource, provider resource, AWS account, region, native currency, source version, canonical records, workload observations and schedule. Source hashes bind the snapshot to the persisted ingestion job. PostgreSQL forces tenant isolation and rejects snapshot update or deletion.

The private Python `/v2/evaluate` worker retains the historical contract for fixtures and replay; public version-2 uploads cannot authorize live execution or verified claims. It remaps original provider records and rejects changed canonical identities, timestamps or amounts. Fourteen complete local calendar days of actual billing and workload coverage establish the baseline; declared day counts and completeness flags have no authority. Explicit zero activity is valid. Missing activity is unknown. Covered or unknown commitment exposure blocks execution.

API uploads are labelled `CUSTOMER_IMPORT`. Persistence proves their immutability, not that AWS issued the invoice or that an independent workload collector observed the activity. Such uploads remain provisional and cannot make live execution ready or establish verified savings. A trusted provider export/invoice reader and independently authenticated workload collection remain required live integration gates. Fixture producer provenance is accepted only in fixture mode; logical fixture time is rejected in live mode. No submitted trust/completeness flag can bypass this gate.

Measurement starts after activation, references that baseline, and requires fourteen complete local days. Matching weekday/time windows use historical effective and contracted economics. Full-day activity must also be preserved, including work outside the scheduled idle windows. The financial window includes the startup lead through required availability. Actual compute, startup costs and attributable incremental charges reduce savings; retained storage and IP charges receive no avoided-cost credit. Reductions beyond executor-owned downtime are confounded. Manual shutdowns cannot supply ownership.

Version 2 conservatively required complete compute-record coverage and supplied contracted economics. It cannot prove omitted stopped intervals from ordinary sparse exports. Version 3 replaces those prerequisites with native pricing and authoritative export/receipt evidence; historical v2 records are not relabelled as v3. Read-only discovery/inspection is usable without financial integrations; a real stop remains blocked until its current v3 gates pass.

Invoice identity, issuance time, finality and at least 99.5% reconciliation are separate verification gates. Reconciliation uses the persisted source's canonical total, not a submitted reconciliation ratio. Unknown inventory costs display as unavailable. Estimated annual run-rate and verified period savings are separate values. Corrections append superseding ledger entries, preserve negative results and cannot overlap an existing resource/time claim.

## Migration and recovery

Legacy imports and ledger entries remain inspectable. Legacy evidence cannot authorize new stops or new verified claims under v3. The console identifies unbound historical entries and excludes them from current verified totals. No historical entry is rewritten.

An already-owned restoration remains authorized independently of the new evidence gates. Pause, approval expiry and evidence migration cannot strand the instance. A consumed restoration cannot start a later manually stopped instance.

## Deterministic acceptance clock

Fixture source data carries an explicit logical financial clock. Its baseline is 1–14 September 2026, activation is 15 September, and measurement is 16–29 September. A completed actual fixture provider cycle is mapped to one logical owned downtime interval: 16 September 20:00 through 17 September 06:45 UTC. Other intervals retain normal compute charges. Quarter-hour records preserve the same workload throughput as the hourly baseline. The normal validator derives £5.16 of synthetic period savings, including running startup time. The immutable mapping retains actual executor receipt timestamps alongside logical timestamps.

This clock is fixture-only. Version-3 sparse-export unit fixtures omit forty-three quarter-hour compute rows inside the owned interval, prove those intervals through a finalized export and reconcile the whole synthetic monthly invoice; they derive the same £5.16 without zero-cost billing rows. A live customer must collect genuine history, wait for the real subsequent observation period and satisfy billing finality. There is no commissioning bypass.

Use [sandbox deployment readiness](sandbox-readiness.md) for account inputs and rollout gates, and [acceptance](acceptance.md) for recorded checks. No AWS resources are provisioned by these checks.

The hosted credential design follows [AWS cross-account IRSA guidance](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/eks/latest/userguide/cross-account-access.html): trust exact service-account identities in the platform account and scope their role assumptions to the customer roles. [AWS Budgets documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/budgets-managing-costs.html) documents delayed actual/forecast notifications; these monitor costs and do not replace the deployment estimate and approval gate.
