# Commissioning dossier

Native hardware and fleet/GitOps components retain separate commissioning gates;
see [hardware/capacity](native-hardware-capacity.md) and
[fleet/local Kubernetes limits](fleet-and-kubernetes-components.md). Optional
hardware IAM/database principals, exact read bindings and candidate allowlists
must be reviewed before enabling that reader. The
[fleet-history records and optional reader](fleet-history.md) retain point
membership and independent completed-work provenance without proving historical
interval ownership or financial authority. The separate private
[hardware identity derivation](hardware-identity.md) preserves archived evidence;
latest-start and ENI attachment facts do not establish creation or ownership
intervals. It has no enabled customer process. Commission the exact fleet identities,
external completed-work meaning and required observation history before using
them in any future action-specific baseline. The
[encrypted local PostgreSQL recovery drill](postgres-recovery-lab.md) proves
quarantined database readback only; it does not establish production recovery
times, object/analytics recovery, identity backup or resumed workflow safety.

This dossier collects the customer gates for the full preserved product contract. It is a preparation record, not permission to provision, spend, enable Autopilot or mutate a customer workload. Engineering continues while these inputs are unavailable. No cloud resources have been provisioned by the extended delivery increment.

The release candidate is the exact tested head of the existing pilot pull request. Its acceptance record, immutable progress reviews and all 233 requirement gates are maintained in [acceptance](acceptance.md), [progress](progress-ledger.json) and [traceability](traceability.md). Record the approved commit and container digests here before commissioning; a moving branch is insufficient. Native telemetry controls and remaining bounds are documented in [native telemetry](native-telemetry.md), and supervised AWS preparation remains in [sandbox readiness](sandbox-readiness.md).

The first hosted cell uses two separate sandbox accounts in `eu-west-1`: the control plane account and the qualifying workload account. The initial deployment is read-only, Autopilot disabled, with execution identities absent. Native global billing API reads do not move product storage or processing out of Ireland. Provisioning requires authenticated account access, expected account IDs, reviewed Terraform plans with no unintended deletion, OIDC configuration, operator CIDRs, EU image registry, encrypted remote state, secret sources, evidence retention and a reviewed [Ireland sandbox cost estimate](sandbox-cost-estimate.md) with approved actual quantities, usage allowances and retained evidence/key obligations. Its reference subtotal excludes unknown charges and is not a spending ceiling. Budget notifications monitor the approved spend; they do not approve deployment.

Consolidate these customer decisions for the final safe handover:

- Exact account and repository access; approved read-role and deployment pipeline identity bindings, external IDs and protected branches.
- Workload owners, explicit environment/classification, configuration authority, statelessness, dependency consumers and action-specific delegation.
- Business calendars, blackouts, known seasonal events, hard SLOs and regression allowances, required availability, RTO and RPO.
- Retention, data-residency requirements, incident contacts, budget/spending ceilings, approval separation and recovery takeover responsibility.
- Independent instrumentation locations and subjects, actual metric definitions and units, health endpoints, source/export ownership and invoice/commitment scope.
- Least-privilege execution approval for one exact supervised canary, its rollback, expiry and immediate pause afterward.

Before a first action, select an existing qualifying resource with genuine complete history or collect the required days. There is no commissioning bypass. Validate authentic source versions, delivery policy, independent observations, ownership, recovery and current safety. Demonstrate backup restoration, key rotation and the applicable incident/recovery procedures before their acceptance credit. A signed plan is not a customer deployment attestation; a pipeline success is not application readiness.

For fleet financial commissioning, a current ASG member list and current tags do
not prove historical workload ownership. Preserve each instance incarnation and
complete historical membership/change coverage before freezing a financial
baseline; periodic point snapshots leave unobserved churn unknown. A controlled
topology needs a complete, independently retained change journal, or an approved
native historical source must prove the intervals. Independently bind the
business demand definition at workload level: per-member HTTP/SLO counters do
not automatically measure preserved business work. Explicit observed zero demand
is valid, while missing observations and unsupported normalization remain
blockers. The scheduling counterfactual cannot be reused for an ASG fleet.

Native account-rate samples support a conditional planning estimate. They do not
guarantee future provider prices or a hard cloud-spend cap. Review the surge,
retained-control and recovery cost exposure with that limitation visible before
approving a fleet program. A future fleet financial scope must cover the whole
owned program and prevent overlap with other action families; the current
private health failure latch alone is not financial ledger enforcement.

The private local Terraform attestor and rate broker require separate production
integration review. A local built-in-provider plan does not attest a cloud
address, and current member billing does not prove historical ASG ownership.
Select the customer pipeline's exact remote Git/state authority, approved
encrypted artifact destination, machine subject and native dimension/rate scope
before connecting either component to a fleet program.

The [group configuration API and console](fleet-configuration.md) record declared
repository, pipeline and Terraform addresses against a registered fleet and a
sealed history scope. They create blocked reviews only. Customer review must
establish native ARN/address mapping, exact remote Git/state authority and
independent evidence before any later executable plan family is commissioned.
The current individual-resource FleetPlanV4 contract has no group bridge; a
representative member or invented EC2 resource cannot satisfy that requirement.

For allocation commissioning, confirm the fixed-share recipients and the current
resource/workload mapping. A sealed current-ownership report is an observed-cost
view with provisional finality, not historical chargeback or verified savings.
Unknown amounts, unallocated charges, credits and each native currency need
explicit customer review before any business chargeback process is accepted.

After an approved intervention, retain actual work-preservation and cost evidence through the complete observation period. Keep results provisional until native invoice finality, commitment exposure, workload normalization, attributable transition/continuing costs and reconciliation gates pass. Rollback permanently excludes the affected measurement from positive verified savings. Customer usability, security assurance and sustained production operation remain separate acceptance gates. Only evidence of the complete contractual workflow in customer use can earn 100%.
